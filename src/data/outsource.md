# Outsource

**Role:** Multimedia Designer, Branding, UX/UI & Motion
**Client:** Outsource Consultants
**Timeline:** 2017 to 2018 (about a year and a half)
**Platforms:** Web, Print, Internal Tools, Motion

## Overview

Eighteen months as Multimedia Designer rebuilding a permit expediting firm's brand, websites, internal tools, and three subsidiary identities.

## Background

After my Ironlak internship I packed up and moved to Brooklyn, and Outsource became my first full-time design job in New York City. I found the role, applied, and interviewed at their Manhattan office, where they gave me a design challenge on the spot: create a graphic for the company's 25th anniversary. I designed a typographic "25" with the industries Outsource serves built into the letterforms, and that piece ended up being part of what got me hired.

## The Company

Outsource is a permit expediting firm based in New York City. They work with architects, developers, and building owners to navigate the complex process of construction filings, code reviews, and permit approvals. It is a highly detail driven industry, and the brand needed to reflect the professionalism and clarity that their clients expected.

## The Starting Point

When I joined, the websites felt like relics. The digital presence across the parent brand and its subsidiaries had not kept pace with the company, and there was no cohesive visual system tying everything together. BuildingOwl had some existing UX and branding work done, but the rest largely needed to be rebuilt from the ground up. My role sat on the marketing team alongside another designer, two developers, and a small marketing crew, all working out of the Manhattan office.

## Reimagining the Brand

Across my year and a half at Outsource I worked on the parent brand and three subsidiaries: Outsource CEU, BuildingOwl, and OSINYC. For the parent brand I redesigned logos, refreshed the color system, and rebuilt the website with a modern visual language. I researched design forward sites on Awwwards to understand what elevated digital experiences looked like, and brought that sensibility into how we approached layout, hierarchy, and interaction across all the properties. Tradeshow materials, social media graphics, digital brochures, and flyers all came out of this work.

### Light and Dark

The mobile experience was designed for both light and dark contexts, with the visual system flexing to maintain hierarchy and brand presence across either mode.

## Outsource CEU

Outsource CEU was a subsidiary built around continuing education for architects. In New York, architects are required to regularly complete accredited coursework to maintain their certifications, and CEU was the platform delivering that content. I built the brand identity for CEU from scratch and designed the digital interfaces that translated dense regulatory and code related material into something clear and approachable. I also produced motion graphics for the CEU video series, which were the educational modules themselves, walking architects through building code topics needed to renew their credits. Brochures and social content supported the brand on the marketing side.

## BuildingOwl

BuildingOwl was a SaaS product built to help building owners and managers track compliance, code requirements, and the lifecycle of their properties. I worked on the product UI, marketing site, brand guidelines, and a printed booklet introducing the platform to prospective clients.

### Booklet and Brand Guidelines

A printed booklet introduced BuildingOwl to prospective clients, and a brand guidelines system codified type, color, and logo usage so the product team could extend the identity consistently.

### Growth Flow Map

A long form internal strategic document mapping how BuildingOwl would expand its user base across building owners, managers, and code reviewers.

## Internal Tools

One of the more interesting UX challenges at Outsource was the internal project tracking software. Employees used it to manage all the information tied to a specific building or job: code requirements, review statuses, building details like when a water heater was last replaced, and anything else relevant to an active filing. The existing interface felt as dated as the public facing websites. I led a visual redesign that modernized the experience and made the information easier to navigate, which was my first real deep dive into UX/UI thinking at a professional level.

## 25th Anniversary

The typographic "25" I designed during my interview, with the industries Outsource serves embedded into the letterforms. It became part of the anniversary campaign and a small reminder that the work you make to get a job sometimes ends up being the work you make on the job.
